How It Works

A reactive, event-driven pipeline powered by Spring Boot, Apache Kafka, Redis, and Google Gemini AI.

01

Upload & Extract CV

Upload your text-based PDF resume (up to 5MB). Apache PDFBox extracts the raw text and Gemini AI parses your skills, experience, projects, and education into a structured profile.

02

Concurrent Scraping

Our search microservice runs parallel scrapers across JustJoin IT, BulldogJob, and Pracuj.pl using Java 21 Virtual Threads, filtering by your selected tech, experience level, and work mode.

03

AI Recruiter Evaluation

Gemini LLM acts as an expert technical recruiter, comparing candidate competencies against vacancy criteria, checking city distances/commutes, and assigning a 0-100 score with concise rationale.

04

Real-Time Streaming & History

Analyzed offers are published via Kafka and streamed immediately to your browser through Server-Sent Events (SSE). Results are persisted in PostgreSQL for ongoing tracking and filtering.

Suitability Score Tiers

Every vacancy is rated and categorized so you can focus on high-probability opportunities first.

80 – 100%

Top Match

Outstanding alignment between your technical stack, seniority, and location requirements. Immediate application recommended.

70 – 79%

Strong Match

Core technologies match well with minor skill gaps (e.g. niche libraries or secondary tools) easily covered on the job.

50 – 69%

Moderate Match

Partial overlap in frameworks, or differences in required years of experience / commuting distance.

0 – 49%

Low Match

Significant divergence in required programming language, specialized framework, or incompatible work location.

Resume Upload Guidelines

Ensure smooth AI analysis by adhering to the following document requirements:

✓

Text-Searchable PDF

Upload a clean PDF exported directly from Word, Google Docs, or Canva. Selectable text allows 100% extraction accuracy.

!

No Password Protection

Encrypted or password-protected PDF files cannot be analyzed by our automated engine. Please remove any passwords.

!

Avoid Image-Only Scans

Scanned camera photos or pure raster image PDFs lack machine-readable text layers. Ensure text can be highlighted.

✓

Size Limit: Max 5MB

Files up to 5MB are accepted and cached in Redis for fast multi-batch evaluation against concurrent scrapers.
