Resume Upload Guidelines

Ensure smooth and accurate analysis by following these recommendations:

✓

Text-Searchable PDF

Upload a clean PDF exported directly from Word, Google Docs, or Canva. Selectable text ensures maximum extraction accuracy.

!

No Password Protection

Encrypted or password-protected PDF files cannot be analyzed. Please ensure password protection is removed.

!

Avoid Image-Only Scans

Scanned camera photos or pure raster image PDFs lack selectable text layers. Ensure your document text can be highlighted.

✓

Size Limit: Max 5MB

PDF documents up to 5MB are accepted for fast, high-accuracy candidate profile parsing and instant matching.
